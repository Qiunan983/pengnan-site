Questions buyers ask first

Is it really free?

The goods are free. You pay the courier, because international courier is charged per parcel and we cannot absorb it on a no-commitment sample. That courier amount is credited back in full on your first order.

How long does the sample take to arrive?

Dispatched within 48 hours of the courier payment clearing. DHL to the US and Canada is typically 4–7 days, Europe 4–6 days.

Can I choose the colours?

Yes. Balloons come in 6 colours at the same price, table covers in 5. Tell us in the notes field and we will pack those.

What is the minimum order after the sample?

USD 500 per shipment, any mix of SKUs. Balloons, glue dots and arch strips can share one carton; table covers, banners and pumps ship in their own cartons.

Can you put my label on the product?

Yes — one colour, one position, applied free of charge. Send a vector file (PDF or AI) and we will show you a proof before printing. Retail-ready barcodes can go on the same label.

Do you dropship or hold stock for me?

No. We ship from China on a per-order basis, air freight, 7–12 days door to door after production. If you need US-based inventory we are not the right supplier yet.
